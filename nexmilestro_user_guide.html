1. Executive Summary & Value Proposition

NexMilestro is an enterprise-grade project and business operations suite engineered by NexSolve Training & Consulting. It unifies prospect lead tracking, field site inspections, milestone lifecycles, and multi-bank accounting under a single web application.

2. Architecture & Data Ownership

NexMilestro runs 100% inside your organization's Google Workspace environment. All data is saved inside your private Google Sheet database and Google Drive with zero external server hosting fees.

3. Module 1: Organization & Company Setup

3.1 Company Branding & Logo Upload

  1. Navigate to Administration → Company Setup.
  2. Click Choose File under Company Logo & Branding.
  3. Select your PNG or SVG logo file and click Save Company Setup.

3.2 Multi-Bank Accounts Management

  1. Click + Add Bank Account.
  2. Enter Bank Name, Account Number, IFSC code, and branch details.
  3. Set primary account tag. Configured banks automatically link across Finance ledgers.

4. Module 2: Lead Management & 1-Click WhatsApp

Track customer inquiries from lead capture to conversion.

⚡ One-Click "Convert to Customer": Click the blue User-Plus icon on any lead row to automatically create an active customer record and transfer all inquiry notes into your Customer ledger instantly!

5. Module 3: Site Visits & Billable Inspection Logs

Log site visits, mark billable inspection fees, and dispatch formatted WhatsApp visit summary updates to client groups in one tap.

6. Module 4: Projects & Milestone Lifecycles

Track project timelines, client budgets, milestone completion progress bars, and vendor allocations.

7. Module 5: Commercial Proposals & Estimates

Prepare client quotes and link approved estimates directly to project milestone budgets.

8. Module 6: Vendor & Associate Directories

Maintain contact records and supply lines for contractors, tradespeople, and independent specialists.

9. Module 7: Finance Booking & Cash Flow Ledger

9.1 Non-Project Expense / Income Toggle

Use the toggle switch at top-left of the booking form to book office overheads (Rent, Salaries, Utilities) without linking a project.

9.2 Dynamic Masters Categories

Categories dynamically populate based on your Masters tab configuration for Income Category (Project), Income Category (Non-Project), Expense Category (Project), and Expense Category (Non-Project).

10. Module 8: Analytics & Financial Reports

Monitor real-time income, operational expenses, project profit margins, and lead conversion rates.

11. Module 9: User Seats, Roles & License Upgrades

Enforce active user seat limits and submit instant 1-click license extension requests directly to NexSolve Training & Consulting.

12. Module 10: Masters Configuration

Manage all dropdown options centrally under Administration → Masters.
