Support Portal

1. Organization & Company Setup

Configure your studio branding, legal tax details, and multiple bank accounts.

  • Upload Company Logo: Select your PNG/SVG logo file. It renders dynamically beside the header text.
  • Tax & Legal Registrations: Enter official PAN & GSTIN numbers for automated invoice compliance.
  • Multiple Bank Accounts: Click "Add Bank Account" to add HDFC, ICICI, or SBI accounts with IFSC codes and tag your primary account.

2. Leads & WhatsApp Integration

Track prospect leads from initial contact to conversion, with 1-click WhatsApp follow-ups.

  • Lead Follow-up Log: Record follow-up dates, discussion notes, and next action items.
  • Send WhatsApp: Click "Send WhatsApp" on any follow-up card to auto-draft standardized customer messages.
  • Mobile vs Desktop Routing: Opens WhatsApp Web on PCs and direct wa.me app on mobile devices.
  • Convert to Customer: One-click conversion transfers lead data directly to your active customer ledger.

3. Site Visits & Billable Notes

Log site inspections, billable visit charges, and dispatch project updates.

  • Visit Purpose & Remarks: Document site measurements, progress checks, and contractor notes.
  • Billable Visits: Tag billable inspection charges to auto-populate into Finance Booking.
  • WhatsApp Visit Update: Send structured "Visit Update" summaries to client project groups.

4. Projects & Milestones

Manage end-to-end client project lifecycles, milestone payments, and vendor costs.

  • Milestone Tracking: Define project phases, due dates, and associate cost allocations.
  • Proposals & Estimates: Create customer proposals and link approved budgets directly to project milestones.
  • Project Analytics: Monitor project health, completion status, and profit margins.

5. Finance Booking & Bank Linking

Book client receipts, vendor spends, and operational cash flows.

  • Non-Project Expense / Income Toggle: Toggle switch at the top-left of the modal form for general office/overhead spends.
  • Linked Bank Account: Select configured bank accounts (e.g. HDFC Current, ICICI Savings) for clear audit trails.
  • Bank Ledger Filter: Filter income and expense transactions by specific bank account.

6. User Seats & Access Control

Control user seats, staff roles, and upgrade license limits.

  • Role-Based Access: Assign Super Admin, Admin, Manager, Designer, or Staff roles.
  • Active Seat Limit: Enforces active user caps configured in license settings.
  • Upgrade User Seats: Click "Upgrade User Seats" in Users & Access tab to request user limit extensions.
